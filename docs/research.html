Research

Current work

Immune control of heart regeneration. Newborn mammalian hearts can regenerate after injury; adult hearts cannot. My work shows that the PD-1/PD-L1 checkpoint pathway maintains the immunosuppressive environment this regenerative window depends on, and asks how T cell populations are recruited and licensed during neonatal cardiac injury.

Self-renewal and ageing of tissue-resident macrophages. During my PhD I characterised a short isoform of Piwil2 required for the self-renewal of murine macrophages, and contributed to work on how alveolar macrophages retain their epigenetic identity through long-term expansion and how microglia mature in the developing brain.
